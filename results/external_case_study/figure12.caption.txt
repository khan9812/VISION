Figure 12. Application to the four external case-study images (006, 026, 066, and 076, respectively). (a) Original images corresponding to all analysis panels in the same column. (b) Projected particle-area distributions and segmentation overlays. Total counts include all detected objects. (c) CLIP object categories and their percentages, calculated over all detected objects. (d) Particle-associated-area distributions and tessellation overlays. The KDE, median, IQR, 95% bootstrap median confidence interval and PF-SUI use all particle-associated areas. The numbers of particle-associated areas (n_PF) are 4, 24, 2, 44 for Cases 1–4, respectively. For readability, the displayed ranges are limited to 0–35,000 pixel² in (b) and 0–200,000 pixel² in (d); the latter is plotted as area divided by 10, with the axis labelled ×10 pixel². The numbers of objects beyond the range in (b) are 0, 1, 0, 2 for Cases 1–4, respectively; the corresponding numbers of particle-associated areas beyond the range in (d) are 1, 1, 0, 0. Values beyond these ranges are omitted only from the displayed windows and remain included in all statistics.
